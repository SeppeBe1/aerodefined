Common questions

More? See the full FAQ.

Is it really free to browse?
Searching, viewing listings and contacting sellers is completely free. We earn only when a deal closes.
What is the commission?
Free accounts pay 2% on completed deals. Premium pays 1.5%.
Can I cancel Premium anytime?
Yes — monthly billing, cancel from your dashboard at any time. No minimum term or cancellation fees.
How many listings can I post?
Free accounts cannot post listings. Premium accounts post unlimited listings across all categories.
