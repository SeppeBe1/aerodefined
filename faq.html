FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about AeroDefined. Can't find what you're looking for? Contact us.

General
What is AeroDefined?
AeroDefined is a B2B aviation marketplace connecting airlines, operators, MRO shops and logistics providers with buyers, freight forwarders and shippers on one verified platform. Browse, contact and trade assets and services directly.
Who uses AeroDefined?
Both sides of the aviation supply chain: sellers include airlines, aircraft lessors, MRO providers, engine traders, cargo operators and logistics companies. Buyers include freight forwarders, charter brokers, importers/exporters, customs brokers and road feeder operators.
Where is AeroDefined based?
We're headquartered in Antwerp, Belgium, with operations across Western Europe. The platform is live across Belgium, Netherlands, Germany, the UAE and beyond.
Pricing & commission
Is AeroDefined free to use?
Browsing, searching and contacting sellers is completely free. You only pay a small commission (2% for free accounts, 1.5% for Premium) when a deal is successfully completed. No upfront costs for buyers.
How much does Premium cost?
Premium is €149 per month, billed monthly. It includes unlimited listings, inbound RFQs, reduced 1.5% commission, verified seller badge, analytics and access to featured placement slots.
Can I cancel my Premium subscription?
Yes, at any time. Monthly billing — cancel from your account dashboard. No minimum term, no cancellation fees. Premium features remain active until the end of the paid period.
What is featured placement?
Premium accounts can boost a listing to the "Featured" row — gold-bordered cards at the top of every search and category page. Maximum 3 featured slots per category at any time, first-come first-served, changeable from your dashboard.
Do you offer Enterprise pricing?
Yes. Enterprise includes everything in Premium plus volume-capped commission, API/ERP integration, a dedicated account manager, SLA guarantees and custom contract terms. Contact us to discuss.
For sellers & operators
How do I list an asset or service?
Create a Premium account, then use the "Post listing" button in your dashboard. Fill in details — category, description, photos, specs and pricing — and your listing goes live within minutes after a quick verification check.
How many listings can I post?
Premium accounts can post unlimited listings across all categories — aircraft, engines, parts, MRO services, cargo capacity, leasing, RFS and more. Free accounts cannot post listings.
What does the Verified badge mean?
The green "Verified" badge means we've confirmed your company identity, VAT registration and aviation operator credentials. Verified sellers receive significantly more enquiries as buyers trust verified listings more.
How do I receive inbound RFQs?
Premium accounts receive inbound RFQs directly to their dashboard. When a buyer posts an RFQ matching your categories, you'll get a notification and can respond with a quote directly on the platform.
For buyers & forwarders
How do I find what I'm looking for?
Use the search bar on the homepage or marketplace to search by aircraft type, engine model, route or part number. Filter by category, region and seller type in the sidebar. Or post an RFQ and receive quotes from verified sellers directly.
What is an RFQ?
RFQ stands for Request for Quotation. Describe what you need — asset type, specs, timeline, budget — and post it. Verified sellers matching your requirements respond with quotes directly.
How do I contact a seller?
Click "Contact seller" on any listing. You'll need a free account (takes under 2 minutes). Once logged in, send messages directly — no phone calls, no broker required.
