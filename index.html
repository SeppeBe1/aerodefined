Process

Up and running in a day

No long contracts, no sales calls. Create your profile and start connecting with the market.

01

Create your profile

Register as buyer, seller, or both. Verify your company — EORI, IATA code, or operator certificate uploaded in minutes.

02

Browse or post

Search live cargo capacity, services, and rates — or post your own listings to start receiving inbound RFQs immediately.

03

Request quotes

Send RFQs to multiple providers at once. Compare rates, lead times, and certifications side by side on one screen.

04

Book & close

Confirm, sign digitally, pay securely. AWBs and cargo documents are generated and sent automatically.
