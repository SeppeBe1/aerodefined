About AeroDefined

Built by people who know
the real cost of fragmented logistics.

We founded AeroDefined because we saw firsthand how air cargo deals were still being made over phone calls, spreadsheets, and email chains — while every other industry had already moved to digital platforms.

Our mission

Close the gap between aviation and logistics.

Airlines, freight forwarders, handlers, truckers, and brokers all need each other — but they've never had a single place to connect efficiently. That costs time, money, and capacity that could be used better.

AeroDefined is that place. A marketplace where buyers and sellers in air cargo logistics find each other, transact digitally, and build long-term relationships — without the friction.

We focus specifically on the operators that enterprise tools like Aeroxchange ignore: smaller forwarders, regional carriers, independent handlers, and growing logistics companies.

What we stand for

Our values

01

Transparency first

Rates, capacity, and provider credentials are visible to everyone. No hidden fees, no opaque middlemen taking invisible cuts.

02

Built for operators

We design for the people actually moving cargo — not for procurement departments. Speed and simplicity over feature bloat.

03

Small and large, equal access

A one-person forwarder in Antwerp deserves the same tools as a global 3PL. Enterprise pricing and walls are not our model.

04

Digital without losing the human

We automate the paperwork, not the relationships. Every transaction still has real people behind it — we just remove the friction.

Our story

How we got here

2023

The problem becomes personal

Working in air cargo, we watched a forwarder lose a client because a last-minute charter couldn't be arranged fast enough. The tools simply didn't exist to connect the right people in time.

2024

AeroDefined is founded

Three co-founders — backgrounds in aviation, logistics, and technology — come together with one goal: build the marketplace the industry actually needs.

2025

Platform goes live

After months of development and conversations with operators across Europe, AeroDefined launches its first version. Cargo booking, brokerage, charter, and RFS — all in one place.

Now

Growing the network

We're onboarding airlines, GSSAs, forwarders, and handlers across Europe and the Middle East. Every new operator makes the marketplace more valuable for everyone on it.
