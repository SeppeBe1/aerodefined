About us

Built by aviation people,
for aviation people.

AeroDefined was founded in Antwerp to solve a real problem: aviation and logistics still rely on fragmented phone calls, emails and Excel sheets. We're changing that.

Our mission

One platform for the entire aviation supply chain.

The global aviation marketplace has always been opaque, slow and broker-heavy. Buyers struggle to find verified assets. Sellers waste weeks chasing unqualified leads. We built AeroDefined to make it transparent, direct and fast.

From aircraft trading and leasing to MRO, cargo capacity and road feeder — AeroDefined brings every layer of the aviation and logistics chain onto one verified platform.

Our values

What we stand for.

01

Transparency first

No hidden fees, no anonymous listings, no opaque commissions. Every seller is verified, every price is visible, every deal is traceable.

02

Direct connections

We remove unnecessary intermediaries. Buyers and sellers negotiate directly, keeping value in the deal rather than paying it away to brokers.

03

Industry expertise

We understand EASA, IATA, DGR and AOC because our team comes from aviation. The platform is built for professionals, not generalists.

Our story

How we got here.

2023

The idea

Frustrated by how fragmented aviation trading is, the idea for AeroDefined is born in Antwerp. First conversations with operators confirm the problem is real.

2024

Building the platform

Development starts. First designs, first user interviews, first verified operator accounts. The marketplace concept is validated.

2025

Beta launch

AeroDefined launches in closed beta across Belgium, Netherlands and Germany. First aircraft deals, first cargo bookings, first verified sellers.

Now

Growing the marketplace

Expanding across Europe and the Middle East. 900+ listings, 480+ verified operators, and growing.
