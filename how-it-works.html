FAQ

Common questions

Do I need an IATA number to sign up?
No. Shippers and importers can sign up without any industry certifications. Freight forwarders and airlines verify their IATA credentials during onboarding.
How does pricing work?
Joining is free. AeroDefined earns a small commission (1.5–2%) only on completed transactions. There are no monthly fees for buyers.
How fast can I get a quote?
Search results are real-time for published capacity. For RFQs, most providers respond within 4–24 hours depending on the route and commodity.
Is my company data secure?
Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We're GDPR compliant and never share your data with third parties without consent.
Can I use AeroDefined for DG cargo?
Yes. Providers list their DG capabilities and class authorizations on their profile. You can filter search results to show only DG-certified providers.
What if a booking goes wrong?
Our support team is available to help mediate any disputes. All bookings are documented digitally, creating a clear audit trail for both parties.
