How it works

Connect. Trade. Close.

AeroDefined connects aviation operators and logistics partners — whether you're buying an aircraft or booking cargo capacity.

01
Free

Create your free account

Sign up in under 2 minutes. No credit card required. Free accounts search, browse and contact any seller — paying nothing upfront.

02
Search

Search or post an RFQ

Find exactly what you need by aircraft type, engine model, route or part number. Or post an RFQ and let verified sellers come to you with quotes.

03
Connect

Contact sellers directly

Send enquiries directly — no intermediaries. All communication stays on the platform, keeping your deal history organised and auditable.

04
2% commission

Close the deal

Negotiate and manage documents digitally. AeroDefined charges a small 2% commission only on completed transactions — nothing until a deal is done.

01
Premium — €149/mo

Create a Premium account

Upgrade to Premium to start listing your assets. Includes unlimited listings, inbound RFQs, verified badge, analytics and featured placement access.

02
List

Post your listings

Create detailed listings for aircraft, engines, parts, cargo capacity, MRO services or logistics offerings. Add photos, specs and pricing — live within minutes.

03

Push a listing to the featured row — gold-bordered cards at the top of every search and category page. Max 3 featured per category, first-come first-served.

Receive leads and close deals

Get inbound enquiries and RFQs directly in your dashboard. Manage conversations, share documents and close deals — just 1.5% commission on completed transactions.
